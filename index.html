EXTRA GAMES COMMUNITY RULES

Rules

These rules apply to accounts, messages, communities, game uploads and reports.

01

Be respectful

No harassment, threats, targeted abuse, bullying or repeated unwanted contact.

02

No illegal or harmful content

Do not upload or distribute illegal material, malware, scams or content intended to cause harm.

03

No spam or impersonation

Do not flood messages, manipulate communities, scam users or impersonate another person or organization.

04

Respect privacy

Do not publish private information about other people. Do not use Extra Games to organize harassment.

05

Report honestly

Use reports for genuine rule violations. Do not submit false reports or fabricate evidence.

06

Moderation decisions

Extra Games may remove content, restrict accounts, close communities or take other moderation action when rules are broken.
